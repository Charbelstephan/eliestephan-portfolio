BRIDAL MAKEUP & BEAUTY

THE ARTIST

Elie Stephan is a Beirut-based makeup artist specializing in bridal and high-profile beauty. Since 2003, he has crafted refined, luminous looks for brides, public figures, and international publications.

With a background in graphic design and photography, Elie approaches beauty with a trained eye for balance, light, and detail — creating timeless results designed to last beyond the moment.

Based in Beirut and working across the region, he offers a personalized, high-touch experience for brides seeking elegance without excess.
